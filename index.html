About

Statistician focused on rigorous methods and practical research support.

My research focuses on survival analysis, including interval-censored data, cure models, multi-state modeling, longitudinal data analysis. I currently work as a research assistant and statistical consultant at the Center for Applied Statistics and Data Analysis (CASDA) at the University of Missouri, where I have supported more than 40 interdisciplinary projects for graduate and faculty, involving study design, statistical modeling, and results interpretation.

In summer 2025, I interned as a biostatistician at Novartis, developing interval-censored multi-state methods for longitudinal clinical trial data, LDL-C levels, and evaluating observation-interval choices in trial design. Outside statistics, I enjoy exploring new places and trying new experiences, including hiking, scuba diving, traveling to different areas, and learning new swimming styles. I value curiosity, adaptability, and continuous learning in both research and life.
